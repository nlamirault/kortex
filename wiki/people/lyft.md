Lyft is a US ride-sharing company whose engineering team created and open-sourced [Envoy](../projects/envoy.md),
the high-performance edge and service proxy that became the data plane for [Istio](../projects/istio.md)
and the foundation of the Envoy Gateway project.

## Key Contributions

- **Envoy** (2016) — open-sourced after internal development; now a CNCF graduated project
- **Envoy Mobile** — Envoy brought to iOS and Android
- Co-contributor to **Istio** service mesh data plane
