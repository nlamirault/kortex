SoundCloud is the music streaming platform where [Prometheus](../projects/prometheus.md) originated in 2012,
developed by Julius Volz and Björn Rabenstein to replace their Borgmon-based monitoring.
Prometheus was open-sourced in 2015 and donated to the CNCF, where it became the second graduated project.

## Key Contributions

- **Prometheus** (2012–2015) — originated the pull-based metrics model, PromQL, and the Alertmanager
