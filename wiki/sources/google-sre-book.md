The Google SRE Book defines the practice of Site Reliability Engineering, including the SLI/SLO/SLA framework,
error budgets, toil elimination, and the principles behind running large-scale production systems reliably.

## Key Chapters

- **Chapter 4** — Service Level Objectives: defines SLIs, SLOs, and the error budget model
- **Chapter 6** — Monitoring Distributed Systems: the four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation)
- **Chapter 13** — Emergency Response and On-Call
- **Chapter 18** — Software Engineering in SRE
